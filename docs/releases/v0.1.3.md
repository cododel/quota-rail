## What's new

- The rail and settings control now have a permanent, subtle outline. Quota Rail no longer captures your screen or asks for Screen Recording permission.
- Settings lists all installed supported CLIs. Providers with quotas follow the order on the rail, followed by providers without quota data and then those that need sign-in.
- Claude and Antigravity quota checks now run in Swift, removing the app's dependency on Python.

## Upgrading

- Subscription visibility and ring order start from their defaults after this update. You can set them again in settings and on the rail.
